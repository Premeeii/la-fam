# Application Overview
La Fam is a web application designed to help friends and families organize shared events, 
manage group schedules, and track shared expenses in one centralized place.
It allows group members to access shared information, coordinate activities, 
and keep track of expenses without relying on a single person to maintain all the records.

## Problem Statement
Friends and families frequently organize activities, appointments, and events together, 
often involving shared expenses.

However, managing these activities and expenses can become difficult when information is scattered across different 
platforms or maintained separately by individual members.
Common problems include:

- Missed Appointments: Group members may forget scheduled activities or have difficulty keeping track of agreed-upon plans.

- Fragmented Information: Event details and schedules may be scattered across conversations or personal calendars, making them difficult to access consistently.

- Disorganized Expense Tracking: Shared bills and expenses may be recorded inconsistently, making it harder to review past transactions and understand group expenses.

## Purpose
La Fam aims to improve coordination among group members by providing a centralized space for shared schedules, events, and expenses.

Instead of relying on one person to maintain and distribute information, the application allows members of a group to access shared records in one place.
The goal is to make group activities easier to organize, shared information easier to access, and expense records easier to manage.

## Goals
### User Goals
-Make shared events and appointments easier to organize and keep track of.

-Provide group members with a common view of schedules and relevant event information.

-Make shared expense records more organized and accessible.

-Reduce reliance on individually maintained records and scattered information.

### Project Goals

- Develop a functional full-stack web application that addresses the identified problems.

- Deploy the application to a publicly accessible production environment.

- Allow other users to explore and try the application beyond a local development environment.

- Gain practical experience in production deployment, monitoring, maintenance, and application reliability.

## References & Inspiration
- Trello: Inspired by its clean interface, flexible content organization, and reusable interaction patterns.

- Discord: Inspired by its group-oriented experience and interface patterns that help users navigate shared spaces and communicate within communities.
